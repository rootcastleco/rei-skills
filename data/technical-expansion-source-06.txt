# Rootcastle CAD & Electronics technical expansion (official Autodesk Fusion + KiCad sources, November 2026)
# Format: @domain|title|category|risk|inputs|units|baseline|hazards|operational boundary
@circuit-architecture|Electronic Circuit Architecture|development|critical|component datasheets, load profiles, schematic files, power trees, input requirements and wiring diagrams|volts, amperes, ohms, watts, hertz and degrees Celsius|approved electrical requirements and a measured or calculated reference circuit|overcurrent, incompatible logic, unstable feedback and wiring reversals|Do not connect unverified electronics to vehicles, mains or machinery; approve voltage ratings, isolation and safe failure states before energizing.
power-tree-budgeting: Sum worst-case load and startup currents per supply rail, then check upstream regulator headroom and dissipation.
ldo-dropout-analysis: Validate regulator dropout over battery voltage, transient load and junction temperature from specified data.
buck-converter-selection: Size the inductor, switch current and input capacitance against ripple and worst-case transients.
boost-startup-sequencing: Check startup current limiting and output overshoot before connecting sensitive downstream loads.
analog-front-end-bias: Calculate sensor source impedance, input bias current and offset propagation for the selected amplifier.
opamp-stability-margin: Evaluate closed-loop gain and output capacitive loading against amplifier bandwidth and phase-margin evidence.
adc-reference-network: Bound voltage reference noise, input settling and source impedance against ADC sample-and-hold timing.
mosfet-gate-drive: Calculate gate charge, transition loss and driver peak current across supply and thermal conditions.
reverse-polarity-protection: Compare diode and ideal-MOSFET protections for reverse connection, inrush and power loss.
isolated-interface-barrier: Establish isolation creepage, rated working voltage and return paths from the required equipment environment.
rs485-bus-termination: Check differential termination, fail-safe bias and ground reference across cable length and node count.
can-transceiver-protection: Review CAN common-mode limits, bus protection and termination against anticipated wiring faults.
sensor-bridge-conditioning: Size instrumentation amplifier gain and common-mode window for a ratiometric bridge sensor.
esd-tvs-coordination: Select protection standoff, clamp voltage and parasitic capacitance against component absolute maximums.
connector-pinout-assurance: Compare electrical net names with physical mating connector orientation and alternate part drawings.
design-power-derating: Compute component power, voltage and temperature derating with documented environmental assumptions.

@circuit-simulation|Circuit Simulation and Verification|testing|safe|SPICE netlists, KiCad schematics, component models, oscilloscope data and supply specifications|volts, amperes, seconds, hertz, degrees and decibels|reproducible nominal circuit simulation with verified component model assumptions|convergence failure, idealized sources, omitted parasitics and model-validity gaps|Simulation alone does not certify safety, EMC or physical circuit operation; measure the circuit before production use.
spice-operating-point: Solve DC node voltages and branch currents, cross-checking bias and device operating regions.
spice-transient-startup: Simulate startup, load steps and source droops while checking numerical step and initial-condition sensitivity.
spice-small-signal-ac: Generate gain and phase over frequency, validating model linearization and excitation assumptions.
spice-noise-budget: Integrate output-referred or input-referred noise over the specified bandwidth using realistic model parameters.
spice-monte-carlo: Explore component tolerance distributions with fixed random seeds and report yield confidence intervals.
worst-case-corner-sweep: Sweep supply, temperature and nominal device extremes with traceable parameter ranges.
opamp-loop-gain-test: Evaluate loop gain or injection-method evidence for feedback stability without relying on closed-loop peaking alone.
switching-regulator-transient: Compare overshoot, ripple and recovery under worst-case current step and load behavior.
adc-sampling-settling: Simulate source RC and hold capacitor charging to the required conversion error bound.
esd-pulse-model-limits: Distinguish behavioral transient circuit stress models from laboratory IEC immunity evidence.
spice-model-library-audit: Check semiconductor model provenance, supported simulator syntax and unknown device subcircuits.
sensitivity-component-rank: Rank output sensitivity to component variation using parameter sweeps and dimensional scaling.
filter-transfer-verification: Compare hand-derived transfer function poles and zeros against simulated Bode response.
thermal-electrical-iterate: Recalculate temperature-dependent conduction loss and junction temperature with explicit heat-path assumptions.
simulation-to-bench-parity: Align simulated node waveforms with calibrated oscilloscope captures under identical stimulus and load.
spice-regression-harness: Create deterministic input decks and pass-fail measurement assertions across simulator version changes.

kicad-library-versioning: Preserve symbol and footprint library provenance while upgrading a shared KiCad project.
kicad-board-stackup: Validate dielectric thickness, copper weight and impedance constraints against a fabricator stackup.
